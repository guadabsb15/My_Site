I am a Research Software Engineer (RSE) at the [Juelich Super Computing Centre](https://www.fz-juelich.de/en/ias/jsc) in Forschungszentrum Juelich. Before that, I was an RSE at the [Rosseland Centre for Solar Physics](https://www.mn.uio.no/rocs/english/) (RoCS) which is part of the Institute of Theoretical Astrophysics at the University of Oslo. My interests include computational sciences, algorithms implementations, and high performance computing.   

During my PhD I was in the [Physics and Astronomy](https://www.stonybrook.edu/commcms/physics/) department at Stony Brook University working with the nuclear astrophysics group. As part of my research, I workied on hydrodynamics simulations of white dwarfs merging using the AMR code [Castro](http://amrex-astro.github.io/Castro/). I contributed to the efforts of including an MHD solver in Castro.

Other non academic interests include swiming, dancing and learning about different cultures.

I did my underdraduate studies in Physics at [Universidad del Valle de Guatemala](https://www.uvg.edu.gt/) and earned a [Global UGRAD](https://exchanges.state.gov/non-us/program/global-undergraduate-exchange-program-global-ugrad) scholarship to do my sophomore year abroad at [North Central College](https://www.northcentralcollege.edu/) in Naperville, IL.
